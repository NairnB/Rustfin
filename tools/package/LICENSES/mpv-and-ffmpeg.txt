mpv-winbuild-cmake project (https://github.com/shinchiro/mpv-winbuild-cmake), release 20260919
(mpv-dev-x86_64-20260919-git-e76a35ec95.7z, sha256 822edd64fcfbe6cc9d596893de5ed10be01dfda76b87ab03132ee19bede6ff83).
